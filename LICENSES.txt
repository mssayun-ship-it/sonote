World map (Trips)
  Map outlines: Natural Earth (public domain), packaged via world-atlas (ISC License).

Exchange rates (Expenses)
  Live rates are fetched from https://www.exchangerate-api.com (open access endpoint): "Rates By Exchange Rate API".
  When offline, built-in approximate rates based on World Bank annual average official exchange rates
  (indicator PA.NUS.FCRF, CC BY 4.0, https://data.worldbank.org) are used until live rates can be downloaded.
